## Ticket One: Resolved
User Problem: User noticed a yellow triangle next to their sound icon and reported not being able to hear anything on the computer.

Steps Taken: Win + X > Device Manager > Audio inputs and outputs. Then you find the yellow triangle next to the corresponding sound driver and right click. Click update drivers.

Outcome: Drivers successfully updated and audio was restored. The yellow triangle disappeared.

Next steps: None required. Ticket Closed.
