Hello Emily! I've heard you've been having internet problems recently. I've diagnosed the issue and already fixed it. Listed within this document is the way on how you can fix it yourself if it happens again.

Win + I -> Systems -> Scroll down to Troubleshoot -> Other Troublshooters -> Network and internet -> Run. This is windows built in troubleshooter. If this does not fix it follow the steps below.

Step 1: press the Windows button either on your taskbar or on your keyboard and type in cmd. Right click it and click "Run as Administrator"

Step 2: Type in ipconfig /release and press enter. Wait 10 seconds.

Step 3: Type in ipconfig /renew and press enter. Wait 30 seconds.

Step 4: Your IP should say 10 and not 169. Check to see if you can search things up in your browser again.

If the problem continues even after implementing this fix, note the number that it says if there is no 10 within the info given within CMD and go to the IT department or call the helpdesk at [NUMBER] for further help.
